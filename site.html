FAQ

General Question

Have questions? Check out our FAQ for quick answers on safety, bookings, and park rules. We’re here to ensure your visit is safe, fun, and hassle-free!

What are the park hours?

Our trampoline park is open from 10 AM to 10 PM, Monday through Sunday. 

What should I wear?

We recommend wearing comfortable athletic clothing and non-slip socks, which can be purchased at the park if you don’t have your own.

Are there age restrictions?

We have designated areas for different age groups to ensure safety. Children below 6 years have their designated zone.

Can I bring food and drinks?

Outside food and beverages are not allowed, but we have a snack bar offering a variety of refreshments.

What safety measures do you have in place?

We prioritize safety with padded walls, non-slip socks, and regular equipment inspections. Our trained staff are always on hand to enforce safety rules and assist guests.

Is there a weight limit?

Yes, our trampoline park has a weight limit of 100 kgs to ensure the safety of all guests. Please contact us for further details.
